# TanRyuGram Partnership Outreach Package

## Positioning

TanRyuGram should present itself as an early-stage creator social and community platform, not as an established company with verified scale. The public site is `https://tanryugram-njs4tc3o.manus.space/`. The support request is specific: startup credits or technical guidance for hosting, storage, bandwidth, domain costs, and reliable operation while the product grows. The message should ask for eligibility guidance rather than demand free services.

Do not state user counts, monthly activity, revenue, funding, partnerships, or engagement figures unless they are verified and supplied separately. Do not send the full source code, database export, user data, or migration archive to prospects.

## Priority prospects

| Priority | Prospect | Best route | Why it fits | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cloudflare for Startups | Official application; public program contact is `startups@cloudflare.com` | The official program lists a bootstrapped/self-funded tier and credits for qualifying technology startups, plus developer-platform and network services. | It requires a public website, a business email matching the domain, a for-profit company, and other eligibility conditions. Domain registration itself is excluded from credits. [1] |
| 2 | AWS Activate Founders | Official AWS Activate application | AWS lists a self-funded Founders tier with up to $5,000 in promotional credits for eligible startups. | The page says the AWS account must be on a paid tier and credits are subject to AWS terms. Do not assume approval or zero billing after credits are exhausted. [2] |
| 3 | DigitalOcean Startups | Official application; public startup contact is `startups@digitalocean.com` | It offers startup credits, support, and a founder community, and says applicants without a VC, incubator, or accelerator can select “Other.” | The program prioritizes AI-native startups, requires a company website and company email, and credit amounts vary. [3] |
| 4 | Google for Startups Cloud | Official application | Google lists a pre-funded MVP path and larger credit paths for eligible early-stage startups. | The larger amounts are not automatic; eligibility and stage requirements apply. Use the official application rather than an unsolicited sales email. [4] |
| 5 | Microsoft for Startups | Official Microsoft startup portal | Microsoft advertises startup credits and technical resources that may help with future infrastructure. | Apply through the portal; do not use a generic support email or claim eligibility before review. [5] |
| 6 | GitHub for Startups | Official application or partner route; public startup contact is `startups@github.com` | GitHub offers credits and developer tooling for eligible partner-affiliated startups. | The official page says outside funding and partner affiliation are required, so this is lower priority unless TanRyuGram joins an accelerator or startup partner. [6] |

## Application notes rather than unsolicited emails

Google for Startups, Microsoft for Startups, and AWS Activate should primarily be approached through their official application portals. A generic email is less effective and may be routed incorrectly. Prepare the same truthful facts for those forms: product URL, founder identity, self-funded status, current technical needs, company/legal status if available, and a short explanation of how credits would reduce infrastructure risk.

## Sending protocol

Send no more than three individually addressed messages initially: Cloudflare, DigitalOcean, and the relevant AWS application route. Do not send the same email to multiple addresses, do not attach user data or source code, and do not follow up repeatedly. A single polite follow-up after seven to ten business days is sufficient. Any request for payment, credentials, or private user data should be treated as suspicious and verified through the provider’s official website.
