# Tanryugram Major Feature Verification Report

- **Stories System**: Verified horizontal story bar with gradient rings, story upload via file picker, full-screen viewer with 5-second progress bar and auto-advance sequence navigation, 24-hour expiration filtering, and live viewer list counters.
- **Audio & Video Calls**: Verified 1-on-1 audio/video call entry points in messenger, incoming call accept/decline banners, WebRTC peer connection setup with STUN servers, mute, speaker output adjustments, front/back camera switching (`facingMode`), call end state persistence, and call history chips.
- **Six Facebook Reactions**: Verified long-press and hover reaction picker with 👍 ❤️ 😂 😮 😢 😡, scale animations, top reaction summary pills, and dedicated reaction detail modal showing reactor names and avatars.
- **Swipe-to-Reply & Message Reactions**: Verified swipe right gesture and tap reply to quote messages, long-press emoji quick reactions, and per-user ("Delete for me") plus everyone ("Delete all") message removal.
- **Voice Notes**: Verified microphone permission handling, media recorder audio/webm recording, live timer and waveform progress bars, and slide-to-cancel gesture support.
- **Multi-Image Carousel**: Verified multi-image file picker supporting up to 10 images per post, swipeable carousel with dot indicators, tagged users metadata, location input, and feeling/activity fields.
