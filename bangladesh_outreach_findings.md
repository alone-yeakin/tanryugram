# Bangladesh Outreach Findings

## Startup Bangladesh Limited

Official website: https://www.startupbangladesh.vc/

The official site identifies Startup Bangladesh Limited as a venture capital company and exposes an official “Submit EOI” route, application-for-investment route, enquiries route, and contact information pages. This is a better fit for an application or expression of interest than an unsolicited generic email. The recommended outreach is to ask which official route fits an early-stage self-funded creator/social platform, without claiming eligibility or requesting guaranteed funding.

## Accelerating Bangladesh

Official website: https://acceleratingbangladesh.com/

The search result identifies the site as an early-stage startup acceleration program in Bangladesh. The page did not provide reliable extracted contact details in the browser session, so no guessed email address should be used. Use only a verified contact or application form from the organization’s official site.

## Outreach safety

Only use official application forms or public organization addresses verified from the organization’s own website. Do not send the same message to many unrelated companies. Do not claim user numbers, revenue, funding, traction, or partnerships that have not been verified. Bangladesh-focused outreach should ask for mentorship, startup-program eligibility, cloud/hosting guidance, or a small pilot partnership rather than assuming an investment decision.

## iDEA Project

Official application portal: https://apply.idea.gov.bd/

The iDEA portal identifies itself as the official application portal for startup grants, SME support, and coworking space. It exposes a Startup Grant application, SME Grant application, coworking application, and startup-grant documentation. This should be handled through the official application portal rather than an unsolicited email. The portal is associated with the ICT Division of the Government of Bangladesh.

## DEIED / Bangladesh Hi-Tech Park Authority

Official website: https://deied.bhtpa.gov.bd/

DEIED lists programs including the University Innovation Hub Program, early-stage and growth-stage startup support, accelerators, investor-connect/funding routes, and infrastructure programs. The official contact shown on the site is info@deied.gov.bd and +880 2-9143026, with ICT Tower, Agargaon, Dhaka-1207. A concise eligibility and partnership inquiry is appropriate; do not claim that TanRyuGram qualifies for a specific program before review.

## Verified contact routes

Startup Bangladesh official contact page: https://www.startupbangladesh.vc/contact/contact-info/

Startup Bangladesh lists general enquiry as info@startupbangladesh.gov.bd and investment enquiry as investment@startupbangladesh.gov.bd. Its official investment page says it targets Bangladesh-registered startups or enterprises with high growth potential or significant social impact, an MVP, and a demonstrated customer base/revenue track record. The official route is the investment application/EOI form, with email used for questions rather than as a substitute for the application.

Official iDEA portal: https://apply.idea.gov.bd/

The iDEA portal offers Startup Grant, SME Grant, coworking-space application, and startup-grant documentation. Use the official portal rather than guessing an email address.

Official DEIED site: https://deied.bhtpa.gov.bd/

DEIED lists early-stage, growth-stage, university innovation, funding, accelerator, infrastructure, and investor-connect routes. It publicly lists info@deied.gov.bd and +880 2-9143026. It also lists technology-support sectors including mobile applications, cloud services, entertainment, and gaming, which are relevant categories for TanRyuGram. [1] [2] [3]
